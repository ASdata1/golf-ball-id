7 iron, indoor, one TP5 vs one Srixon Soft Feel, strictly alternating (protocol deviation D14). 24 shots, 23 valid (shot 23 GC3 spin misread).

| Measure | TP5 (n=11) | Soft Feel (n=12) | Cohen's d | p |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ball speed (mph) | 120.1 | 120.0 | 0.03 | 0.94 |
| Launch (deg) | 17.5 | 17.1 | 0.54 | 0.21 |

| Carry (yd) | 171.2 | 170.8 | 0.07 | 0.87 |
| Spectral centroid (Hz) | 4135 | 4030 | 0.26 | 0.55 |
| High frequency energy share (>5 kHz) | 0.029 | 0.021 | 0.44 | 0.32 |

Club speed, attack angle and path were the same for both balls, so the swing was consistent across the alternation.

**Power (back spin, 80% power, α = 0.05):** ~10 shots per ball for 500 rpm, ~26 for 300 rpm, ~104 for 150 rpm, using the raw SD of 386 rpm. Adjusting for club data cuts the SD to ~315 rpm.

**Conclusion:** no detectable difference on full 7 irons, and differences above ~300 rpm look unlikely. See D16 for what changes.
